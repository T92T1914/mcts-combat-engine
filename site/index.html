More search work

On the four frozen deciding cases, 64 simulations reduced pooled exact one-round modeled loss by 74.65% compared with 8. Of 64 seed pairs, 22 improved, 3 worsened and 39 tied. The adverse exploratory E2 case remains visible.

Eight times the declared simulations is a larger allowance. This finite-corpus finding does not establish a same-work algorithm improvement or stronger full-encounter play.

Same-work search time

Request-local action reuse lowered search-only API time by approximately 11.75% under the declared paired aggregate on one shared Windows host. Two of 30 individual pairs were slower.

Simulation work, actions, random draws and payoffs were preserved. This does not establish lower complete-command latency, GPU performance or stronger decisions.

A decision engine I can inspect
