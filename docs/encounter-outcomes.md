# Complete encounter outcome evaluation

The accepted first action reference advantage did not produce a demonstrated terminal win advantage in this prespecified encounter study. The result is inconclusive about the direction of the underlying difference. Default UCT remains unchanged.

The new capture command retains complete encounter records and actual work through the separate installed companion. See [the consumer guide](encounter-policy-evaluation.md) for acquisition, execution, inspection and resource boundaries.

## Frozen experiment

The study used the exact shipped duel, gauntlet and boss presets. Each decision requested 32 simulations and 32 transitions at horizon one. Both policies used the existing reward and simulator, retaining their own final selection treatments. Search was serial and clockless, with exploration 1.2 and no priors. Every encounter had a fresh managed owner and a maximum of 30 rounds.

The exploratory phase contained four independent seed blocks and 24 encounters. Its cost, complete work and retained directory sizes selected the largest feasible even deciding count under the prospective rule. The deciding phase contained 374 independent seed blocks and 2244 encounters. No deciding seeds were replaced. The pilot outcomes did not select the policy, tune it or determine the sample size.

Environment and search seeds were independently drawn with replacement from separate declared ranges. Each block paired the starting labels across both policies and all three presets. The RNG objects were separate. Different actions can consume different later random events, so the starting pairs do not establish matched trajectories.

The primary endpoint was a terminal win by round 30. An ongoing round cap was a nonwin for that endpoint and remained separately labeled. Operational failures would have left outcomes unknown and withheld primary inference. This completed collection had zero operational unknowns and zero work shortfalls.

## Observed outcomes

Every row contains 374 encounters. These preset counts are descriptive.

| Preset | Policy | Terminal wins | Terminal losses | Ongoing round caps |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Duel | UCT | 308 | 12 | 54 |
| Duel | Sequential halving | 301 | 11 | 62 |
| Gauntlet | UCT | 312 | 46 | 16 |
| Gauntlet | Sequential halving | 307 | 53 | 14 |
| Boss | UCT | 84 | 232 | 58 |
| Boss | Sequential halving | 73 | 228 | 73 |

UCT won 704 of 1122 encounters. Sequential halving won 681. The equal weighted estimate of halving minus UCT terminal win probability was -23/1122, or -2.0499 percentage points.

For each block, the primary analysis averaged the three paired binary win differences. Integer moments were N=374, S=-23 and Q=223. The unbiased block variance was 82873/1255518. The single prespecified empirical Bernstein interval, with alpha 0.05, was [-11.4652, +7.3654] percentage points. It includes zero. This does not establish equality, absence of benefit or underlying harm.

The interval derives from [Maurer and Pontil, Theorem 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0907.3740), using the bounded block average and allocating half the error allowance to each tail. Exact integer moments and conservative rational numerical enclosures were fixed before collection. There was no postcollection switch of statistical method.

The prior one round reference finding remains accepted within its own objective and inputs. This encounter study measures terminal wins for three fixed modeled presets. It supplies no full encounter optimality claim, broad scenario generalization or default promotion.

## Work and cost

Every admitted decision completed its 32 simulation and 32 transition allowance. UCT used 779840 transitions over its encounters. Halving used 797952, an excess of 18112, approximately 2.32 percent. Longer encounters changed the number of decisions. Equal per decision allowances did not produce equal total simulator work.

The halving treatment recorded 511 uniform fallback decisions, all in gauntlet. This is the existing declared fallback for larger root action inventories. All other decisions used the declared halving mode, which includes roots with a single legal action.

The collector measured 605.219 seconds for the deciding capture. The separate external owner measured 605.531 seconds, including its own boundary overhead, and confirmed retirement with an empty Windows Job. The independent reconstruction took 71.078 seconds under that owner. Child timing includes interpreter startup, content loading, recording, search, environment execution and close. These local Windows wall measurements are descriptive and do not establish a same trajectory speed improvement.

The retained owner enforced 512 MiB aggregate committed memory, declared wall limits and a five second retirement allowance. The collector itself records a caller supplied external ownership declaration. Its declaration does not verify enforcement. Temporary transport reads are bounded, while temporary file writes have no independent disk quota.

## Evidence and reuse

The [frozen protocol](encounter-study/protocol.json), [method contract](encounter-study/method-contract.md), [paired outcomes](encounter-study/paired-outcomes.csv), [independent analysis](encounter-study/analysis.json) and [projection manifest](encounter-study/manifest.json) retain the deciding inputs, integer moments, preset outcomes, adverse block labels, work and timing. The CSV has all six binary outcomes and their integer block sum. A reader can reconstruct S, Q and the stated mean and variance from those rows.

The public files are a projection of the accepted local collection. The manifest records LF normalization for the Markdown method contract. Its frozen source already used LF, so that operation left its bytes unchanged. All 2244 complete episodes, receipts and raw diagnostics remain in the private acceptance packet. The public projection is not the raw corpus. The independent verifier checked 15713 retained files and reconstructed terminal classification from final state HP. Process supervision was checked separately through the actual owner receipts.

Original source mode records preserve their full original content and implementation identities. The Windows source checkout used CRLF bytes, while the committed companion uses canonical LF payloads. Installed mode also carries its own import and distribution identity. Exact replay can therefore refuse an original study record under that different context. The record must remain immutable. Passive inspection and a clearly fresh bounded decision from its stored state are separate supported uses.

Fresh records created by the installed engine and companion can be replayed under their matching recorded context without search. See the consumer guide for the identity contract and saved state commands. An installed acceptance fixture is separate from the frozen scientific dataset.
